# 📱 Mobile Store Sales & Consumer Demographics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze mobile store sales performance, customer demographics, payment preferences, and purchasing trends. This project helps businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales and customer insights by analyzing:

- Overall business performance
- Monthly sales trends
- City-wise sales distribution
- Customer demographics
- Payment mode preferences
- Product ratings
- Day-wise sales performance

The dashboard is fully interactive, allowing users to filter data by **Year**, **Brand**, **Mobile Model**, and **Month**.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 📅 Month-wise Sales Analysis
- Track monthly sales performance throughout the year.
- Identify seasonal demand patterns.
- Compare monthly revenue trends.

### 🌍 City-wise Sales Analysis
- Analyze sales contribution from different cities.
- Identify top-performing markets.
- Support regional sales planning.

### 💳 Payment Mode Analysis
- Distribution of transactions across:
  - UPI
  - Debit Card
  - Credit Card
  - Cash
- **UPI** emerged as the most preferred payment method with approximately **26%** of total transactions.

### ⭐ Customer Rating Analysis
- Visual representation of customer ratings (1–5 stars).
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and product quality.

### 📅 Day-wise Sales Performance
- Compare sales across weekdays.
- Identify peak business days.

### 👥 Customer Demographics
- Analyze customer age groups.
- Understand purchasing behavior across different age segments.

### 🎛 Interactive Filters
- Year
- Brand
- Mobile Model
- Month
